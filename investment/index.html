Principles

First, don't lose.

That is the Sanctus principle.

This is our investment principle. It is not a guarantee of returns or principal.

We focus on the risk behind the return.

Venture, mezzanine, pre-IPO, or content investment: whatever the investment, there is risk.

We analyze risks on many fronts, then design and structure each investment so those risks can be hedged before we invest.

In the end, the model we aim to build puts risk reduction first, not high returns with a higher chance of failure.

Investment structure

Sanctus's investment structure is very simple.

But before we make that simple decision, we carry out the most complex analysis and decision-making.

Among venture-stage companies, how many early-stage startups actually survive for ten years?

Sanctus does not focus on the 10x or 100x returns right in front of us.

We look closely at whether the structure and the company allow an exit, and whether there is any risk of failure, even if the multiple is lower.

Six things we check before we decide

  1. 01Every possible risk
  2. 02The company's business model
  3. 03References obtained through the VC network
  4. 04Time to exit
  5. 05The company's value
  6. 06Multiple (return)

These are the criteria Sanctus Investment uses in its review. They are not a guarantee of returns or principal.

What we favor

For these reasons, Sanctus favors pre-IPO investments over early-stage ones, invests in structured finance and mezzanine where risk can be reduced, and prefers the arbitrage or spread income found in supply chain finance and securitization.
