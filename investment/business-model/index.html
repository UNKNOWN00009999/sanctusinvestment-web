Business model

We source alternative investments and traditional assets such as corporate finance, selecting for safety and profitability.

Once we first identify an investment asset, we screen and analyze it, invest, monitor and track it continuously, and then exit, aiming to realize a return.

Business model diagram

Process

  1. 01

    Source investable assets: traditional assets and alternative investments

  2. 02

    Analyze, verify, and remove risk factors

  3. 03

    Negotiate with the deal parties

  4. 04

    Design the deal structure

  5. 05

    Arrange advisory support and check references through our network

  6. 06

    Decide on the investment structure

  7. 07

    Principal investment and fundraising

  8. 08

    Execute the investment: deal closing

After closing

Closing the deal is not the end. After we invest, we keep looking for additional risk factors, and we monitor and watch continuously so we can remove any we find.

Through this process, we work to bring each investment to a successful exit.
