sanctus : holy : ἅγιος : קָדוֹשׁ : set apart : separate : different

The Hebrew counterpart of Latin sanctus, קָדוֹשׁ (qadosh), means "holy," that is, "set apart" and "different."

The name

Sanctus Investment seeks to change the world while in it, as a presence set apart and different from it.

That is why we exist.

Key drivers for choosing Sanctus

01

Family office

Sanctus Investment Co., Ltd. is a family office focused on investing. Our main work is capital-raising advisory, principal investment, and consortium investment.

Our basic investment strategy centers on hedging risk, and we use structured finance to plan our exits.

We adjust risk exposure to each investor's profile, design the investment and deal structure to fit, and pursue strategies aimed at risk-adjusted outcomes.

02

A network in Korea and abroad

We have built a network of institutional investors in Korea and abroad (securities firms, hedge funds, PE and VC firms, accelerators, and others), accounting firms, advisory firms, and listed companies in Korea.

We also actively support value creation to raise the value of the companies we have invested in.

From deal sourcing to advisory support to deal closing, we actively use our network in Korea and abroad.

03

Value add

We also support efforts to raise the value of our portfolio companies through a value-add strategy, alongside our investment in them.
